# Rank Builder SEO Brief

## Positioning

Rank Builder SEO should become a plain-spoken SEO knowledge base with practical how-to articles, testing notes, and eventually a conversion path into the agency.

## Initial Content Pillars

- Technical SEO checks that small businesses can understand.
- Content planning for durable search demand.
- Local SEO setup and maintenance.
- Cloudflare, GitHub, and static-site SEO experiments.
- Analytics and measurement basics.

## Experiment Boundaries

This site can test title patterns, internal linking structures, schema variants, crawl controls, publishing cadence, and AI-search visibility tactics. Keep experiments reversible and record each change in `experiments/registry.md` before or immediately after launch.
